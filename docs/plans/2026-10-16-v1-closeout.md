# v1 close-out: fonts, level meters, mockups, two-thumb review

Owner picked backlog items 2, 4, 5 and 6 (`docs/backlog.md`, v1 close-out).

## Level meters (engine + Mixer screen)
- The audio thread measures the post-fader peak level of each of the 12 mixer channels, the
  delay and reverb returns, and the master (after the limiter).
  - It publishes them through relaxed atomics, with a fast attack and a ~300 ms release, plus a
    clip flag held for 1 s.
  - No allocation, locks or logging; the readings come from the same per-chunk buffers.
- JNI: `NativeEngine.meters(out: FloatArray)` (contract written by the meters agent, appended).
- The Mixer screen draws the live level inside each bar column over the fader-setting marker.
  The selected column stays the left-thumb target (ADR 0003).

## Fonts (app)

## Two-thumb review (app; ADR 0003)
- Review the Synth, Seq and Tape screens against a reach model: left thumb on the left
  ~40% of the width and the lower 75% of the height; right thumb on the right ~40%. Anything
  only reachable at top-centre needs a second, thumb-reachable route.
- Fix what's small and clear. Write the findings, and anything bigger, up as proposals in
  `docs/adr/0003-two-thumb-grip.md` for the owner.
