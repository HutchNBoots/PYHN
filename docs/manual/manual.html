★Top findings so far

Ranked across the sections below; they merge with the consistency review into one list before any build is planned.

  1. High3.2

    The Note strip flattens a multi-step selection to one pitch: drag it with four bass steps selected and all four become A3. Undo recovers it.

    Note moves every selected step by the same interval and reads +2.

  2. High3.2

    Pitch, chords, length, velocity and nudge are invisible at a glance. Reading one step takes a long-press, which can delete it (a wobble becomes a tap) or arm the keys to overwrite it.

    Note names on lit pads, a pitch roll in the display (tap a note to select it), velocity as fill height, tails for long notes.

  3. High3.1 · 3.2

    No copy of a pattern or a page. Bar 2 of a 32-step pattern starts empty; a variation means re-entering everything.

    Shift + tap a page number copies the page; a Dup chip copies the pattern.

  4. High3.2

    Chords need three or four fingers held together; there is no way to add or remove one note.

    While a step is selected, the first key replaces and each further key toggles a note; the keys light the chord.

  5. High1.1

    Any key touch closes the preset strip, so you can't load a preset and play it. Each audition costs another long-press.

    Keys don't close the strip; group labels (Bass, Lead, Pad, Pluck, FX).

  6. High1.4 · 2.2

    Sound edits (synth and drum) have no undo. Double-tap resets to the engine default, not the preset's value.

    One drag = one undo step, as on Seq; double-tap returns to the preset's value.

  7. Medium3.1 · 3.2

    A latched Shift turns the Undo chip into Redo; after Shift + Clear you can't undo without a stretch to Shift.

    Redo only while Shift is held (or a long-press on Undo).

  8. Medium3.1

    Drum names on the phone are initials (DK, CS, TS, Cras, Bloc, Shak); the Drum map is upside down against the Seq grid; the right half of the keys is dead on Drum.

    Hand-written short names; one shared kit grid in thumb reach (owner: Kick bottom-left or top-left?).

  9. Medium3.1 · 3.2

    Live record: drums need the Drum screen (Seq has no drum keys), so you record blind; timing is snapped (nudge 0), held length is dropped, no count-in.

    Arming Record on Seq shows keys or kit pads; optional unquantised record; count-in.

  10. Medium1.2

    Resting a thumb on a strip for half a second locks it, and the drag that follows does nothing.

    Lock on Shift + long-press, or a longer hold with a haptic.

  11. Medium1.3

    Key and scale live only on the Seq Shift layer (7 touches, 2 screen switches); there is no keyboard octave shift (Octave moves the sound and its pattern).

    Key, scale and keyboard octave reachable from the Synth screen.

  12. Medium3.1 · 3.2

    Clear also resets Length, Rate, Gate, Shuffle and motion; pages don't follow the playhead; the triplet grid still groups by 4.

    Clear removes notes only; page follow; beat edges follow the rate.

  13. Low1.3 · 3.1

    Glide does nothing in Poly; the step Length strip does nothing on drums (one-shots); the pad-tap velocity is the last drum key's velocity.

    Dim strips that do nothing; show the entry velocity.

1Synth sounds

Slots 2–8 each hold one synth; slot 1 is always the drum kit. Each synth slot keeps its own sound, strip locks and selected section.

1.1 Pick a sound

Tap a number 2–8 in the top bar, or swipe sideways on the display: finger left goes to the next slot, finger right to the previous one (about 58 dp per slot). The swipe stops at 2 and 8 and never jumps to the drums.

To change the sound in a slot, open its preset strip: long-press the display (easiest for the left thumb) or long-press a slot button 2–8. A row of 16 presets slides over the top row of keys; the current one is coral. Tap a name to load it. The strip closes when you touch anything else.

1.2 Shape it

The rail on the left picks a section: Osc, Filter, Env or Mod. The four strips show that section's controls; tap Shift (top right) to latch the second layer, tap again to go back, or hold it for a momentary layer. Shift is shared by every screen, so a latched Shift stays on when you change screens.

Strip gestures: drag up or down (the full range is 1.5 strip heights, so it never jumps to your finger); a second finger resting on the strip gives ¼ speed; double-tap resets to the built-in default; a 0.5 s still hold locks the strip (padlock).

1.3 Play it

The keys cover two octaves, C3 to B4. Where you hit a key sets its velocity: near the top is soft, near the bottom is loud. To play higher or lower, change the sound's Octave (Osc · Shift · strip 1); this moves everything the slot plays, its pattern too, and the key labels still read C3 / C4. Key and scale are one per session and set on the Seq screen (Shift, no step selected, strips 3 and 4); back on Synth, out-of-key keys dim and play nothing and roots get an amber stripe.

1.4 Keep it

Everything about each synth slot saves by itself, about a second after you stop changing things and when the app goes to the background: every value, the preset it came from, the selected section and the locks. To get back to a preset, open the strip and tap it again; that throws away every edit on the slot. You can't save your own preset, name a sound, or copy a sound to another slot.

3.1A drum beat

Everything happens on the Seq screen with slot 1 selected. Left: the page rail (1–4) and Clear. Display: the 4×4 sound grid, the 4×16 overview and the footer with Undo. Right: four strips. Bottom: 16 step pads.

Lanes. You work on one sound at a time. Tap a sound in the grid (you hear it) and it becomes the lane. Pads show that sound: bright where it plays, a small dot where only other sounds play, dark where empty. Tap a pad to add or remove the lane's sound; other sounds on the step stay (up to 8).

Worked proposal: “How do I double my beat?”

Today: drag Length 16 → 32, tap page 2, re-enter every hit lane by lane: about 18 touches for a beat, 12–25 for a bassline. Shortening a pattern keeps its hidden steps, so lengthening can also bring old notes back.

  1. ×2 chip (recommended). In the Seq footer beside Undo. Length L becomes 2L; steps 1…L are copied into L+1…2L with every hit value and the motion lanes. Dimmed above 32 steps; works for odd lengths (12 → 24). The playhead keeps its place, so nothing jumps; the pads move to the new page and flash “×2 · 32 steps”. One undo step. Shift + ×2 halves. 1 touch, left thumb, no Shift.
  2. Page copy (with it). Shift + tap a page number copies the page you're viewing there: the tool for “now vary bar 2” or “bar 1 into bar 4”. 1 touch + Shift.
  3. Lengthening repeats automatically (not recommended). No new control, but hidden, and you can't make empty space on purpose.
  4. Double all slots (later). Shift + long-press ×2. Useful before a tape commit, heavy-handed otherwise.

Implementation: Kotlin only (PatternStore and PatternHistory, through the existing setters). No audio-callback change.

Worked proposal: “How do I quantise my beat?”

The catch: today you can't play an unquantised beat in the first place. Live recording snaps every hit to the nearest step with nudge 0 and drops held lengths, so quantising has two parts: let recording keep the feel, then give a one-touch way to tighten it. Our hits already have nudge (±½ step) and length, so a loose hit is exactly “nearest step + nudge”: no save-format change.

  1. Recording keeps the feel (prerequisite). The engine already knows the exact frame of each played note; it writes the offset as nudge and the held time as length. Arithmetic only on the audio thread (no allocation or lock), one extra field in the recorded-event queue, with a host test that the offset is sample-exact.
  2. Q chip (recommended). In the footer beside Undo and ×2. Tap = tighten 100% (nudge × 0) for the selected steps, else the current drum sound, else the whole synth pattern. Shift + Q = the whole pattern at 50%: tighter but still human. Recorded lengths round to the nearest 1/16. Swing is applied on top, so it stays. The chip glows when there's something loose to tighten. One undo step. 1 touch.
  3. A live Quantise % per pattern (v1.1 option). Dial between loose and tight while listening; nothing is lost. But every strip slot is taken, and “stored vs heard nudge” gets confusing.
  4. Quantise while recording (fallback). Keep today's snap as an option (Shift + long-press Record toggles Snap / Feel). Default: Feel.

The resulting flow for “I jammed a beat and it's sloppy”: play (the feel is kept), then tap Q. Today that's 2 + N touches per lane, and only works on nudges you set yourself.
